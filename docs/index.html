Why clients work with me

Most small businesses do not need a large agency, a bloated platform, or months of abstract discovery. They need someone senior who can listen carefully, understand the business quickly, and turn that understanding into working software.

That is where I fit. I bring more than 25 years of experience across software development, management consulting, product leadership, networking, IoT, and running technology businesses. That mix is unusual: I can sit with a business owner, understand the real operational problem, challenge what does not make sense, and then build the system that solves it.

This is not software for its own sake. My work is about practical outcomes: fewer manual steps, fewer disconnected systems, better data flow, simpler workflows, and tools that people actually use.

How I work

I start with the business problem, not the technology.

  1. Understand what is really happening Who does the work, where information gets stuck, where mistakes happen, and what users will tolerate.
  2. Design the right-sized solution Sensible architecture, maintainable code, and a strong bias toward usability, not complexity for its own sake.
  3. Build something dependable Systems you can use, trust, and build on, not demos that fall apart in production.
  4. Work directly with you Plain-English communication, honest scoping, and no layers of account managers or junior staff.

Background

I have worked in IT for more than 25 years, from hands-on software development through consulting, advisory, product leadership, and business ownership.

My early career included development and technical roles at Logica, Grey Interactive, Spark Response, and Avanade, followed by founding and managing GoneClear Ltd. In Trinidad and Tobago, I held progressive consulting and leadership roles at Infotech Caribbean Limited before spending six years at EY as Manager, Advisory Services, working on operational improvement and profitability projects across multiple industries.

I later served as Product Owner and interim CTO at Resonance Trinidad Limited, helping lead diversification into digital payments and digital products. I am also co-founder and General Manager of WifiVentures Limited, a WiFi, networking, and IoT services company grown largely through client referrals.

I am currently building and promoting EntryZen, a visitor access and security platform for gated communities: a practical example of focused software that solves a real operational problem.

Today, I work directly with clients who need a senior, hands-on technical partner: someone who can understand the business problem, design the right approach, and build the software or integrations needed to make it work.
