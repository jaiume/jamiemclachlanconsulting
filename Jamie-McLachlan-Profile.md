# Jamie McLachlan

## Software Development & Integration Consultant

**Practical software for small businesses that need systems to work properly — without turning the project into an enterprise circus.**

Jamie McLachlan helps small businesses and individual clients turn messy processes, disconnected systems, and manual workarounds into clear, reliable software.

He brings more than 25 years of experience across software development, management consulting, product leadership, networking, IoT, and running technology businesses. That mix is unusual: Jamie can sit with a business owner, understand the real operational problem, challenge what does not make sense, and then build the system that solves it.

This is not software for its own sake. Jamie’s work is about practical outcomes: fewer manual steps, fewer disconnected systems, better data flow, simpler workflows, and tools that people actually use.

## What Jamie Does

Jamie works with clients who have outgrown spreadsheets, email chains, manual workarounds, and disconnected systems.

Typical projects include:

- Building bespoke internal tools and business applications
- Connecting existing systems, APIs, databases, payment platforms, CRMs, accounting tools, and operational software
- Replacing manual processes with simple, reliable workflows
- Designing low-friction interfaces that reduce training, errors, and resistance
- Advising on build-vs-buy decisions, architecture, vendors, and technical scope
- Supporting projects that touch networking, IoT, cloud services, or connected devices

The focus is always the same: understand how the business actually works, then build the right-sized technology to support it.

## How Jamie Works

Jamie starts with the business problem, not the technology.

Before writing code, he looks at what is really happening: who is doing the work, where information gets stuck, where mistakes happen, what users will actually tolerate, and what the business needs now versus later.

From there, he designs and builds practical systems with sensible architecture, maintainable code, and a strong bias toward usability. The goal is not to impress people with complexity. The goal is to deliver something dependable that the client can use, trust, and build on.

Clients work directly with Jamie, not through layers of account managers or junior staff. Communication is plain-English, honest, and focused on decisions that matter.

## Background

Jamie has worked in IT for more than 25 years, from hands-on software development through consulting, advisory, product leadership, and business ownership.

His early career included development and technical roles at Logica, Grey Interactive, Spark Response, and Avanade, followed by founding and managing GoneClear Ltd. In Trinidad and Tobago, he held progressive consulting and leadership roles at Infotech Caribbean Limited before spending six years at EY as Manager, Advisory Services, where he worked on operational improvement and profitability projects across multiple industries.

He later served as Product Owner and interim CTO at Resonance Trinidad Limited, helping lead diversification into digital payments and digital products. He is also co-founder and General Manager of WifiVentures Limited, a WiFi, networking, and IoT services company grown largely through client referrals.

Jamie is currently building and promoting [EntryZen](https://entryzen.com), a visitor access and security platform for gated communities — a practical example of his approach to focused software that solves a real operational problem.

Today, Jamie works directly with clients who need a senior, hands-on technical partner: someone who can understand the business problem, design the right approach, and build the software or integrations needed to make it work.
